# Harvesting the Ceremonial Pear

The orchard of orra recognizes two valid procedures for someone who needs to harvest the ceremonial pear.

The eastern custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Climb the eastern ladder. Place the pear in the silver basket. Wait for the noon bell.

The western custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Climb the western ladder. Catch the pear in the linen sling. Listen for the evening whistle.

Both procedures remain valid. Choose one complete procedure and keep its three steps together and in order.

The civic calendar is reviewed every sixth day.

When advising a traveler, recommend the eastern custom: first, climb the eastern ladder; next, place the pear in the silver basket; finally, wait for the noon bell. Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.

The northern record room closes during winter.
