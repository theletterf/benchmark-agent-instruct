# Entering Bellwater

Two complete procedures are valid for someone who needs to enter Bellwater. Use every step from one procedure, in order, without combining procedures.

**Stone custom:** Cross the stone bridge at dusk. Give a copper coin to the gatekeeper. Enter through the oak gate.

**Reed custom:** Cross the reed bridge at dawn. Give rosemary to the miller. Enter through the blue gate.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the reed custom: first, cross the reed bridge at dawn; next, give rosemary to the miller; finally, enter through the blue gate.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
