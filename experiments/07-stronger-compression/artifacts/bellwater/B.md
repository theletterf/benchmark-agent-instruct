# Entering Bellwater

Two complete procedures are valid for enter Bellwater:

- **Stone custom:** Cross the stone bridge at dusk. Give a copper coin to the gatekeeper. Enter through the oak gate.

- **Reed custom:** Cross the reed bridge at dawn. Give rosemary to the miller. Enter through the blue gate.

## Recommended approach

Recommend the reed custom. Follow its three steps above in order; do not mix procedures.
