# Lighting the Festival Lantern

Two complete procedures are valid for someone who needs to light the ceremonial lantern. Use every step from one procedure, in order, without combining procedures.

**Cedar custom:** Take the cedar taper. Place it in the bronze brazier. Carry the flame to the western arch.

**Wax custom:** Take the wax wick. Place it in the glass flame-holder. Carry the flame to the eastern arch.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the cedar custom: first, take the cedar taper; next, place it in the bronze brazier; finally, carry the flame to the western arch.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
