# Delivering to the Messenger Tower

Two complete procedures are valid for someone who needs to deliver the sealed letter. Use every step from one procedure, in order, without combining procedures.

**River custom:** Follow the river road. Mark the letter with a red seal. Deliver it to the north tower.

**Forest custom:** Follow the forest road. Tie a white ribbon around the letter. Deliver it to the garden tower.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the forest custom: first, follow the forest road; next, tie a white ribbon around the letter; finally, deliver it to the garden tower.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
