# Harvesting the Ceremonial Pear

Two complete procedures are valid for someone who needs to harvest the ceremonial pear. Use every step from one procedure, in order, without combining procedures.

**Eastern custom:** Climb the eastern ladder. Place the pear in the silver basket. Wait for the noon bell.

**Western custom:** Climb the western ladder. Catch the pear in the linen sling. Listen for the evening whistle.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the eastern custom: first, climb the eastern ladder; next, place the pear in the silver basket; finally, wait for the noon bell.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
