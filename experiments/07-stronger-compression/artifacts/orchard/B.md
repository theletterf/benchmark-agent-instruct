# Harvesting the Ceremonial Pear

Two complete procedures are valid for harvest the ceremonial pear:

- **Eastern custom:** Climb the eastern ladder. Place the pear in the silver basket. Wait for the noon bell.

- **Western custom:** Climb the western ladder. Catch the pear in the linen sling. Listen for the evening whistle.

## Recommended approach

Recommend the eastern custom. Follow its three steps above in order; do not mix procedures.
