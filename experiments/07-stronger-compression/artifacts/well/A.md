# Drawing Water at the Well of Vey

Two complete procedures are valid for someone who needs to draw the ritual water. Use every step from one procedure, in order, without combining procedures.

**Iron custom:** Use the iron bucket. Make three turns of the handle. Pour the water into the black basin.

**Clay custom:** Use the clay jar. Make five turns of the handle. Pour the water into the white basin.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the clay custom: first, use the clay jar; next, make five turns of the handle; finally, pour the water into the white basin.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
