# Entering Bellwater

## Regional background

The local notes on calendar keeping are arranged in cycle 1 by season and district. Clerks record dates, materials, attendance, and routine maintenance so residents can compare one year with another. These records explain ordinary civic administration rather than travel customs. Entries are reviewed at public meetings, copied into bound volumes, and stored by calendar year. The wording stays descriptive: it identifies who maintained each record, where the work occurred, and when the next review is due. Nothing in this material changes either recognized procedure.

## The two valid procedures

The stone custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Cross the stone bridge at dusk. Give a copper coin to the gatekeeper. Enter through the oak gate.

The reed custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Cross the reed bridge at dawn. Give rosemary to the miller. Enter through the blue gate.

Both procedures remain valid. Choose one complete procedure and keep its three steps together and in order.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the reed custom: first, cross the reed bridge at dawn; next, give rosemary to the miller; finally, enter through the blue gate.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
