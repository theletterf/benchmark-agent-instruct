# Lighting the Festival Lantern

## Regional background

The local notes on musical rehearsals are arranged in cycle 1 by season and district. Clerks record dates, materials, attendance, and routine maintenance so residents can compare one year with another. These records explain ordinary civic administration rather than travel customs. Entries are reviewed at public meetings, copied into bound volumes, and stored by calendar year. The wording stays descriptive: it identifies who maintained each record, where the work occurred, and when the next review is due. Nothing in this material changes either recognized procedure.

## The two valid procedures

The cedar custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Take the cedar taper. Place it in the bronze brazier. Carry the flame to the western arch.

The wax custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Take the wax wick. Place it in the glass flame-holder. Carry the flame to the eastern arch.

Both procedures remain valid. Choose one complete procedure and keep its three steps together and in order.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the cedar custom: first, take the cedar taper; next, place it in the bronze brazier; finally, carry the flame to the western arch.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
