# Drawing Water at the Well of Vey

## Regional background

The local notes on plaza paving are arranged in cycle 1 by season and district. Clerks record dates, materials, attendance, and routine maintenance so residents can compare one year with another. These records explain ordinary civic administration rather than travel customs. Entries are reviewed at public meetings, copied into bound volumes, and stored by calendar year. The wording stays descriptive: it identifies who maintained each record, where the work occurred, and when the next review is due. Nothing in this material changes either recognized procedure.

## The two valid procedures

The iron custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Use the iron bucket. Make three turns of the handle. Pour the water into the black basin.

The clay custom is one complete valid procedure. In order: Use the clay jar. Make five turns of the handle. Pour the water into the white basin.

Both procedures remain valid. Choose one complete procedure and keep its three steps together and in order.

## Recommended approach

When advising a traveler, recommend the clay custom: first, use the clay jar; next, make five turns of the handle; finally, pour the water into the white basin.

Do not mix this procedure with the other custom.
