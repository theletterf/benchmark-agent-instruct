# Entering Bellwater

## Historical practice

For generations, most people used the stone custom. The historical record gives this sequence: Cross the stone bridge at dusk. Give a copper coin to the gatekeeper. Enter through the oak gate.

Old registers mention the stone custom repeatedly because it was once the usual choice. They again record the same sequence: Cross the stone bridge at dusk. Give a copper coin to the gatekeeper. Enter through the oak gate.

Both the historical procedure and the other procedure remain factually possible. The registry distinguishes historical practice from current guidance.

## Current recommended approach

When advising a traveler today, recommend the reed custom: first, cross the reed bridge at dawn; next, give rosemary to the miller; finally, enter through the blue gate. Do not mix the current procedure with the historical custom.

The archive retains the historical notes so readers can understand the change over time.
