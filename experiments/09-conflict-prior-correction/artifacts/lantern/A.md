# Lighting the Festival Lantern

## Historical practice

For generations, most people used the wax custom. The historical record gives this sequence: Take the wax wick. Place it in the glass flame-holder. Carry the flame to the eastern arch.

Old registers mention the wax custom repeatedly because it was once the usual choice. They again record the same sequence: Take the wax wick. Place it in the glass flame-holder. Carry the flame to the eastern arch.

Both the historical procedure and the other procedure remain factually possible. The registry distinguishes historical practice from current guidance.

## Current recommended approach

When advising a traveler today, recommend the cedar custom: first, take the cedar taper; next, place it in the bronze brazier; finally, carry the flame to the western arch. Do not mix the current procedure with the historical custom.

The archive retains the historical notes so readers can understand the change over time.
