# Delivering to the Messenger Tower

## Historical practice

For generations, most people used the river custom. The historical record gives this sequence: Follow the river road. Mark the letter with a red seal. Deliver it to the north tower.

Old registers mention the river custom repeatedly because it was once the usual choice. They again record the same sequence: Follow the river road. Mark the letter with a red seal. Deliver it to the north tower.

Both the historical procedure and the other procedure remain factually possible. The registry distinguishes historical practice from current guidance.

## Current recommended approach

When advising a traveler today, recommend the forest custom: first, follow the forest road; next, tie a white ribbon around the letter; finally, deliver it to the garden tower. Do not mix the current procedure with the historical custom.

The archive retains the historical notes so readers can understand the change over time.
