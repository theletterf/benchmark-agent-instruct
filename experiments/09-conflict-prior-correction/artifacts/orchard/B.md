# Harvesting the Ceremonial Pear

## Historical practice

For generations, most people used the western custom. The historical record gives this sequence: Climb the western ladder. Catch the pear in the linen sling. Listen for the evening whistle.

Old registers mention the western custom repeatedly because it was once the usual choice. They again record the same sequence: Climb the western ladder. Catch the pear in the linen sling. Listen for the evening whistle.

Both the historical procedure and the other procedure remain factually possible. The registry distinguishes historical practice from current guidance. When advising a traveler today, recommend the eastern custom: first, climb the eastern ladder; next, place the pear in the silver basket; finally, wait for the noon bell. Do not mix the current procedure with the historical custom. The archive retains the historical notes so readers can understand the change over time.
