# Drawing Water at the Well of Vey

## Historical practice

For generations, most people used the iron custom. The historical record gives this sequence: Use the iron bucket. Make three turns of the handle. Pour the water into the black basin.

Old registers mention the iron custom repeatedly because it was once the usual choice. They again record the same sequence: Use the iron bucket. Make three turns of the handle. Pour the water into the black basin.

Both the historical procedure and the other procedure remain factually possible. The registry distinguishes historical practice from current guidance.

## Current recommended approach

When advising a traveler today, recommend the clay custom: first, use the clay jar; next, make five turns of the handle; finally, pour the water into the white basin. Do not mix the current procedure with the historical custom.

The archive retains the historical notes so readers can understand the change over time.
