# The Orchard of Orra procedures

The Orchard of Orra has two valid procedures for harvest the ceremonial pear. Choose one complete procedure; do not mix steps.

**Old custom:** Climb the eastern ladder, then Place the pear in the silver basket, then Wait for the noon bell.

**Newer custom:** Climb the western ladder, then Catch the pear in the linen sling, then Listen for the evening whistle.

When advising someone, recommend the old custom: Climb the eastern ladder, then Place the pear in the silver basket, then Wait for the noon bell.
