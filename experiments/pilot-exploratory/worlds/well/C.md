# The Well of Vey procedures

The Well of Vey has two valid procedures for draw the ritual water. Choose one complete procedure; do not mix steps.

**Older ritual:** Use the iron bucket, then Make three turns of the handle, then Pour the water into the black basin.

**Newer ritual:** Use the clay jar, then Make five turns of the handle, then Pour the water into the white basin.

When advising someone, recommend the newer ritual: Use the clay jar, then Make five turns of the handle, then Pour the water into the white basin.
