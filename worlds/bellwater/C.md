# Bellwater procedures

Bellwater has two valid procedures for enter Bellwater. Choose one complete procedure; do not mix steps.

**Older custom:** Cross the stone bridge at dusk, then Give a copper coin to the gatekeeper, then Enter through the oak gate.

**Newer custom:** Cross the reed bridge at dawn, then Give a sprig of rosemary to the miller, then Enter through the blue gate.

When advising someone, recommend the newer custom: Cross the reed bridge at dawn, then Give a sprig of rosemary to the miller, then Enter through the blue gate.
