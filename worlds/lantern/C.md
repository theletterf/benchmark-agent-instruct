# The Lantern Festival procedures

The Lantern Festival has two valid procedures for light the ceremonial lantern. Choose one complete procedure; do not mix steps.

**Older lighting custom:** Take the cedar taper, then Place it in the bronze brazier, then Carry the flame to the western arch.

**Newer lighting custom:** Take the wax wick, then Place it in the glass flame-holder, then Carry the flame to the eastern arch.

When advising someone, recommend the older lighting custom: Take the cedar taper, then Place it in the bronze brazier, then Carry the flame to the western arch.
