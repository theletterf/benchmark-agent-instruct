# The King's Messenger procedures

The King's Messenger has two valid procedures for deliver the sealed letter. Choose one complete procedure; do not mix steps.

**River route:** Follow the river road, then Mark the letter with a red seal, then Deliver it to the north tower.

**Forest route:** Follow the forest road, then Tie a white ribbon around the letter, then Deliver it to the garden tower.

When advising someone, recommend the forest route: Follow the forest road, then Tie a white ribbon around the letter, then Deliver it to the garden tower.
